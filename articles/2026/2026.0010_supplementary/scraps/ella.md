
#### Ella
The Ella has two views: view A with a V-neck, under the family Argentellidae (@sec-argentellidae); and view B with a curved square neckline, under the family Argenkamellidae (@sec-argenkamellidae).

The Ella is named for a Swedish model named Ella, who Silversaga hired during the photoshoots for the S/S2023 collection. Preorders for the Ella opened on April 3, 2023.

Silversaga began recruiting pattern testers for the Ella on July 1, 2023, and the Ella was released on July 28, 2023. This original pattern is now view A of the Ella dress as it appears in 2026. On September 15, 2023, the Ella's size range was further expanded. On November 4, 2025, Silversaga began recruiting testers for an updated version to add three new sizes and an additional view (View B; the Argenkamellidae), and on December 9, 2025, the updated Ella was released.
