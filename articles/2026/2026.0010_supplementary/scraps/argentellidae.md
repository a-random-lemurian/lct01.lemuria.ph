
## Class Vecostola {#sec-vecostola}
**Vecostola** Lemuria, 2026

## Family Argentellidae {#sec-argentellidae}
**Argentellidae** Lemuria, 2026; familia nova

**Taxonomic placement**: Placed in the class Vecostola.

**Diagnosis**: Phylogenetically, the Argentellidae are characterized by origin from View A of the Ella dress from Silversaga. Phenetically, they are characterized by a V-neck with a ventral medial drawstring, a ventral medial seam inferior to the drawstring, and bilateral seams at roughly 45&deg; angles that meet at the drawstring. Dorsally, the neckline terminates roughly parallel to the scapulae.

**Remarks**: For view B of the Ella, see Argenkamellidae (), under the order Sueciargenta ().

Silversaga also recommends the Ella as a pattern for creating dresses similar to the Venus dress from Christy Dawn (Chrivenusidae Lemuria, 2026; unpub.).
