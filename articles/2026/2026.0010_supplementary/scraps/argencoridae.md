

## Family Argencoridae {#sec-argencoridae}
**Argencoridae** Lemuria, 2026; familia nova

**Description**: Bilateral double straps, reconfigurable. One single continuous rouleau loop with a single tie, typically at dorsal medial point. Ventral medial plunging V-shaped neckline, terminating inferior to bust. Zipper and ties present dorsally. Ventral medial seam and bilateral horizontal seam at tip of neckline. Bilaterally, two darts on fabric, transitioning into gathers towards superior end.

**Remarks**: The Argencoridae, the Cora top, is one of the few discontinued sewing patterns in Silversaga's repertoire. The pattern is attested solely on the Internet Archive. Pattern testing took place during March, and it was eventually released on Etsy. It was discontinued by Silversaga on September 29, 2024, due to it no longer meeting Silversaga's standards [@silversaga2024_nomorecora].
