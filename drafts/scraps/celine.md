## Family Celinidae {#sec-celinidae}
**Celinidae** Lemuria, 2026; familia nova

**Etymology**: From Celine + *-idae*, family suffix.

### Genus *Celina* {#sec-celina}
***Celina*** Lemuria, 2026; gen. nov.

**Etymology**: From Celine, with the ending modified to -a to make it more Latinate.

#### *Celina laurentia* {#sec-celina-laurentia}
***Celina laurentia*** Lemuria, 2026; sp. nov.

**Diagnosis**: *Celina laurentia* is characterized by its fabric, *Tanaflora amelieluisa*, the Amelie Luise Tana Lawn cotton fabric, from Liberty Fabrics & Bridgerton.

**Etymology**: From *Laurentia*, pseudo-Latin feminine form of *Laurentius*, the name from which *Lauren* originated.
